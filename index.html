Monitoring pipeline · real farm data

VeldSentry

The question: Could a potato farm get an early warning before a crop fails?

What I built: A pipeline that tracks crop health across 50 fields every day, combining Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, NASA POWER weather data and modelled soil moisture, with a Streamlit dashboard and SMS alerts. Built on data from a real potato farm in Limpopo's Soutpansberg region.

What it means for you: Data from several messy sources, combined into one view and an alert you can act on.

Open-source tool

Forecourt Scout

The question: Is this address a good site for a petrol station?

What I built: A tool that scores any address from 0 to 100 on six weighted factors, such as road type, competition and demographics, using only open data from OpenStreetMap and the Stats SA Census 2022. I built it for a prospective client's question. The engagement didn't go ahead, so the tool is now open source.

What it means for you: A first screen in minutes, before paying for traffic surveys or market studies.
