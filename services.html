Client Audit · Travel & Lifestyle Account · Anonymized

Finding the real cause of an engagement drop, after two wrong theories

The client suspected dropping hashtags had hurt their engagement rate. Checking their own posting history directly showed engagement had already been declining for 8 months before hashtags stopped — that theory didn't hold up.

The next lead, posting volume, looked promising: it had roughly tripled right as engagement crashed. But digging into the timestamps found that 20% of the "extra" posts were near-duplicate content posted minutes apart — consistent with Instagram's Trial Reels testing feature, not genuinely new content. Removing those barely moved the numbers. Volume wasn't the answer either.

The actual cause: the account had quietly shifted its format mix from roughly a third Carousel posts down to under 10%, and Carousel posts convert reach into engagement several times better than Video. Confirmed with proper statistical testing (not just eyeballing a chart), across hundreds of posts.
