Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This policy covers the Instagram analytics application operated by Ruben Vermaak ("I", "me"). The application connects to Instagram Business/Creator accounts via the Instagram API to produce content and audience performance analysis, either for my own account (Karmic Steps) or for a client's account with their direct, explicit authorization.

What data is accessed

Through Instagram's official API, and only for accounts that have explicitly authorized this application via Instagram's own login flow, the application can read:

  • Basic profile information (username, follower/following/media counts)
  • Media metadata (captions, timestamps, permalinks, media type)
  • Post-level insights (reach, impressions, likes, comments, shares, saves)
  • Comment text and counts on the authorized account's own posts

All requested permissions are read-only. This application never posts, comments, follows, or modifies anything on Instagram on your behalf, and never requests your Instagram password directly — authentication happens entirely through Instagram's own OAuth login.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively to produce content-performance analysis and reports for the account owner: engagement patterns, posting-time analysis, audience insights, and similar descriptive analytics. It is not used for advertising, profiling of third parties, or any purpose unrelated to the account owner's own analytics.

Where data is stored

Data is stored locally on my own equipment in plain files, not on any shared or public server, and is not accessible to anyone other than me. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used to train any third-party product.

Data retention and deletion

For a one-time audit, the associated access token is deleted once the engagement is complete. Underlying account data is retained only as long as needed to deliver the agreed analysis or ongoing reporting, and is deleted on request. To request deletion of your data or revoke access at any time, email rpvermaak123@gmail.com, or revoke the connection directly from your Instagram account's Settings → Apps and Websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled: email rpvermaak123@gmail.com.
